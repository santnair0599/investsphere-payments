# Maintenance SLA Policy (Real Estate)

**doc_id:** maintenance_sla_policy

Work orders are resolved within these SLAs by priority:

| Priority | Resolution SLA |
|----------|----------------|
| CRITICAL | **24 hours** |
| HIGH     | 72 hours |
| MEDIUM   | 7 days |
| LOW      | 30 days |

A **critical** work order (safety, security, major building system) must be resolved
within **24 hours**. Repeated critical orders on the same property, or maintenance cost
rising more than 15% vs the prior window, feed the property underperformance flag.
