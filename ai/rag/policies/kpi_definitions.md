# KPI Definitions

**doc_id:** kpi_definitions

- **Occupancy rate** — occupied units ÷ total units (0–1). *Occupancy risk* = a drop of
  more than 5 percentage points vs the prior 30-day window.
- **RevPAR** (Revenue Per Available Room) — room revenue ÷ rooms available. The core
  hotel performance KPI.
- **Conversion rate** (entertainment) — tickets sold ÷ visitors (footfall). Measures how
  well venue traffic turns into ticketed revenue.
- **Footfall–revenue paradox** — when **footfall rises but revenue falls**, the KPI that
  explains it is the **conversion rate**: more visitors are entering but a smaller share
  are buying tickets, or average ticket value has dropped. Investigate pricing, product
  mix, and campaign targeting.
- **Churn rate** (customer) — inactive contacts ÷ total contacts in a segment.
- **Excess return** (investment) — YTD return minus benchmark return.
- **Quarantine rate** — records failing DQ ÷ records read; a data-trust signal.
